Phone Remote

Scan the QR code in your terminal with your phone.

These are speaker notes. They show up on your phone.

Tap Next or swipe left over the notes. The timer starts with the first “Next”.

Speaker notes

  • Notes of the current slide on the phone
  • Title of the next slide
  • A− / A+ change the notes size
  • Notes can contain HTML: lists, emphasis, …
  • Try A+ to make them larger.

Tap Spot. Touch the pad – everything but a circle dims. Move to point at a box, lift to turn it off.

“Hold” keeps the spot, S / M / L sets its size.

Black screen

“Black” on the phone pauses the deck, “Show” brings it back.

Same as pressing B on the keyboard.
